# Extended toolbox: rescue routes outside geolocation models

Research date: 2026-10-30 UTC. Scope: independent datasets, libraries, websites and workflows; no software installed, no photo uploaded, no payment or account created. GitHub material was retrieved with `gh api`; other first-party documentation and metadata with `curl`. Examples below are proposed workflows, **not claims of completed photo-location tests**. Local captures are under `sources/extended-*`; blocked pages and JavaScript shells are retained as failures, not supporting evidence.

## What is actually new

Inspected `skills/geo-sleuth/references/{search,aerial,sky,data-sources}.md` before researching. Existing capability already includes EXIF extraction, Apple Vision/RapidOCR, perspective/crop/mirror variants, Baidu/Yandex and manual Lens, municipal tree inventories (specifically Vancouver), Commons geosearch, Wikidata P18/class/bbox pools, Overpass, manually viewed OpenRailwayMap/OpenInfraMap, Google/Baidu historical panoramas, Google Earth history, a mention of Esri Wayback, DEM skyline rendering, solar geometry, generic weather checks and commercial flight-history services. Those are **not new discoveries**.

The additions worth prioritizing are deeper independent evidence routes:

| Priority | Addition | Why it rescues a stuck investigation |
|---|---|---|
| First | Municipal asset/permit joins, not just tree species | Pole–hydrant–intersection spacing and construction chronology survive ambiguous names and generic architecture. Verified examples include actual utility inventories, not hypothetical portals. |
| First | Source-date provenance for aerial imagery and web photos | Separates basemap publication, aerial acquisition, upload and archive-crawl dates; prevents rejecting an old scene against a new map. |
| First | GTFS stop codes, destinations and service calendars | A few legible transit characters become a finite set of stops/route corridors, independently of visual geolocation models. |
| First | Historical OSM and local PBF alternatives | Recovers removed/renamed features and survives busy Overpass servers; does not mistake today's mapping for historical ground truth. |
| Next | Commons metadata/license/source expansion | Existing photo pools become auditable original-source leads, including nearby photographs without Wikidata P18 links. |
| Next | Official rail-crossing, aviation and nautical feature datasets | Infrastructure identifiers can be much stronger than vegetation or architectural style. |
| Next | Local multilingual OCR and preserved orientation | Makes another independent recognizer available without uploading sensitive pixels. |
| Conditional | Station weather, tide, Moon/star and high-resolution surface crosschecks | Useful only after a candidate region/date exists; these are consistency tests, not global location oracles. |

## Evidence and privacy contract

For every route, retain query/URL, retrieval time, bbox or IDs, requested historical time, source revision/acquisition time, returned rows, pagination/truncation flags, attribution/license, errors and comparison artifact. Keep the original pixels unchanged and use separate derivative files. A successful HTTP status does not mean a usable response: several official sites here returned HTML shells or challenges.

**Negative evidence must be bounded.** “No hit” means only no indexed record within that service's geography, time, feature classes, filters and completed pagination. Neither an absent private tree, absent historical panorama, unmapped building, missing GTFS shape nor missing archive capture excludes a real location. Permit issue dates are not completion dates. Distinct websites derived from OSM, or several imagery releases using the same source acquisition, are not independent corroboration.

Public-coordinate queries still reveal an area of interest to a service. Prefer metadata-first and small bboxes. Photo upload, even by URL, discloses pixels or the source URL to another operator; obtain approval and inspect current terms before using it. Do not send faces, plates, private interiors or an original GPS-bearing file by default. Public imagery/data access does not imply permission to redistribute tiles, photographs or bulk databases.

## 1. Historical imagery and archival provenance

### A. Esri World Imagery Wayback, with acquisition metadata

**Minimum input:** candidate point/bbox; approximate scene age helps. **New:** existing refs mention Wayback, but do not provide its local-change/source-metadata workflow. The first-party app repository and `@esri/wayback-core` expose `getWaybackItems`, `getWaybackItemsWithLocalChanges` and `getMetadata`. Compare locally changed releases at a consistent scale, then inspect metadata at the point: source acquisition date/resolution/provider matter more than release labels. A release date is **not** the capture date. [App README](https://github.com/vannizhang/wayback), [core README](https://github.com/vannizhang/wayback-core); captures [app](sources/extended-gh-wayback-source.txt), [core](sources/extended-gh-wayback-core.txt).

**Access/rights:** interactive exploration and package documentation are publicly accessible without buying anything in this research; SDK code licensing and imagery usage rights are separate. Check service attribution/terms before exporting or automating tiles. **Recovery:** the app was a JavaScript shell and one Esri blog returned 403, so use the documented core metadata or the interactive app, not scraped blog text. **Negative:** no local change does not mean no physical change; the same acquisition can persist across releases.

### B. Google Earth and Street View history: dates are observations, not a timeline of all reality

**Minimum:** candidate location. Earth historical imagery and Street View “See more dates” are existing capabilities, but the history controls deserve an explicit rescue step before rejecting a facade. Compare permanent features across available dates and save attribution/date/position. Some places have no historical Street View. Do not treat a displayed year as a completion date, or a panorama position as the photographer's position. [Earth help](https://support.google.com/earth/answer/148094?hl=en), [Street View help](https://support.google.com/maps/answer/3093484?hl=en); captures [Earth](sources/extended-earth-history.txt), [Street View](sources/extended-google-history-street.txt). Public browser access; reuse restrictions still apply. Recovery: official imagery history UI rather than undocumented endpoint retries.

### C. USGS EarthExplorer / NAPP: actual historical aerial frames

**Minimum:** US candidate bbox and approximate decade. NAPP ran **1987–2007**; the official archive describes more than 1.3 million frames. Existing medium/high-resolution digital scans can be downloaded without charge; on-demand rescanning is a separate paid service and was **not ordered**. Frame scans are not automatically orthorectified: camera tilt, terrain relief, orientation and scale variation remain. Use frame IDs/footprints to compare road layouts and old industrial sites; georeference carefully before measuring positions. EarthExplorer download/account requirements must be checked at use time. [USGS collection, products and prices](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/eros/science/usgs-eros-archive-aerial-photography-national-aerial-photography-program-napp), [capture](sources/extended-usgs-aerial.txt).

**Recovery:** inspect other dated imagery collections in the official catalog when the date falls outside NAPP; do not claim an unexamined collection covers the target. A frame footprint intersection is not proof the feature is visible/unclouded.

### D. Local historical maps and library APIs

**Minimum:** plausible municipality/old street name. A particularly actionable verified source is Vancouver's `goads-fire-insurance-map-1912-georectified`: footprints, construction materials, old shoreline and recently changed street names, with downloadable ECW/TIFF tiles. It omits the New Westminster volume and Stanley Park. The same city catalog exposes dated LiDAR products. These can recover demolished structures or a pre-land-reclamation shoreline. [Official catalog query](https://opendata.vancouver.ca/api/explore/v2.1/catalog/datasets?limit=30&where=search%28%22water%22%29%20or%20search%28%22street%20lighting%22%29%20or%20search%28%22traffic%20signal%22%29), [capture](sources/extended-vancouver-catalog.txt). Vancouver Open Government Licence; source dates and tile footprints constrain coverage.

The [Library of Congress JSON/YAML API](https://www.loc.gov/apis/json-and-yaml/) is a separate public catalog route for historical map/photo item records ([capture](sources/extended-loc-maps.txt)). Check each item's rights advisory and digitized resources; an API record is not necessarily a downloadable georeferenced image. The Sanborn collection page returned 403 here, so its specific collection coverage was not verified.

### E. Internet Archive: recover the original caption, not just a similar image

**Minimum:** source-page URL or exact image URL, optionally date interval. Query availability, then CDX for both the page and image asset. CDX fields include `timestamp`, `original`, `statuscode`, `mimetype`, `digest`; use date/status filters and `collapse=digest` to avoid repeatedly inspecting identical captures. Replay the page and its actual asset, retaining capture timestamps separately. A page can load an image archived on a different day. [Availability API](https://archive.org/help/wayback_api.php), [CDX upstream documentation](https://github.com/internetarchive/wayback/blob/master/wayback-cdx-server/README.md); captures [availability](sources/extended-wayback-api.txt), [CDX](sources/extended-gh-cdx-readme.txt).

Public reads need no photo upload/account; archive access grants no new copyright licence. **Recovery:** exact asset URL → canonical page → another captured date; respect throttling and robots/access restrictions. The guessed `archive.org/developers/wayback-cdx-server.html` returned 404; upstream documentation was obtained with `gh`. Earliest *available crawl* is not original publication, original photographer or capture date. No archive hit is not proof a page never existed.

## 2. Municipal inventories: independent candidate generation

**Minimum:** municipality plus visible asset constellation or an address fragment. These are city-specific sources, not global coverage. Use the official portal metadata/API, inspect schema/date/ownership and fetch all relevant classes in a bounded area. Then compare spacing, sides of street and feature sequences; do not filter first on uncertain species or equipment type.

| Verified source / clue | Actionable use | Access, rights and failure bounds |
|---|---|---|
| Vancouver `public-trees` | Existing source, now metadata-checked: public boulevard/park trees, genus/species/cultivar/diameter/height and geometry; private trees omitted. | Public API, Open Government Licence–Vancouver. Attributes can lag reality by years; missing-coordinate records are omitted. Not a complete canopy census. [Metadata](https://opendata.vancouver.ca/api/explore/v2.1/catalog/datasets/public-trees), [capture](sources/extended-vancouver-metadata.txt). |
| Vancouver `street-lighting-poles`, `street-lighting-service-panels`, `street-lighting-junction-boxes` | Match a distinctive run of poles plus cabinet/box positions; use published geometry instead of guessing a city's lamp style. | Public catalog, same licence; described as generally survey-accuracy, but updates can lag. Do not infer lamp-head shape or pole height unless actually present in schema. [Catalog capture](sources/extended-vancouver-catalog.txt). |
| Vancouver `traffic-signals`, `water-hydrants`, `sewer-manholes`, `sewer-catch-basins` | Join intersection pattern with hydrant/manhole positions when OSM lacks small fixtures. | Signals are positioned approximately at intersection **centres**, not individual signal heads. Hydrants are municipal water-system inventory; not an inventory of every possible private fixture. Public same-licence data. [Catalog capture](sources/extended-vancouver-catalog.txt). |
| NYC 2015 Street Tree Census `uvpi-gqnh` | Independent city example for street/address/species/tree-position candidate generation. | Public Socrata metadata; 2015 census is not current truth. Retain NYC terms/attribution; do not assume Vancouver's licence applies. [Metadata](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/api/views/uvpi-gqnh.json), [capture](sources/extended-nyc-tree-metadata.txt). |
| NYC DOB permits `ipu4-2q9a` **and** DOB NOW `rbx6-tga4` | Scaffolding, demolition, major additions: join address/job type/permit history, then seek dated official plans or imagery for the visible building. | Public metadata; legacy BIS dataset explicitly warns that most current permits are in DOB NOW. A permit is one work type, can change status and does not prove work occurred. Avoid collecting/reprinting personal applicant/contact fields. [BIS metadata](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/api/views/ipu4-2q9a.json), [NOW metadata](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/api/views/rbx6-tga4.json); captures [BIS](sources/extended-nyc-permit-metadata.txt), [NOW](sources/extended-nyc-now-metadata.txt). |

### Reusable portal-query skills

[Socrata SoQL](https://dev.socrata.com/docs/queries/) supplies selection, filters, grouping and pagination ([capture](sources/extended-socrata-query.txt)); public requests can be throttled, with app-token requirements/quotas dependent on the host. Use deterministic ordering and retain row counts; a default page is not a citywide inventory.

[ArcGIS Feature Service query](https://developers.arcgis.com/rest/services-reference/enterprise/query-feature-service-layer/) supports spatial filters, `outFields`, count/ID queries and pagination where advertised ([capture](sources/extended-arcgis-query.txt)). Read layer metadata first: CRS, `maxRecordCount`, object-ID field, pagination support and `exceededTransferLimit`. Request IDs first and then bounded ID batches when offset pagination is unsupported. Transform CRS explicitly. Public layers may be open; private layers require authorized tokens. Data-owner licences, not the API software licence, govern reuse.

**Recovery:** portal UI shell → public catalog metadata → linked FeatureServer/SoQL/Explore API; check successor datasets and archival extracts. No utility record can exclude a region unless that inventory's ownership, date, precision and completeness actually cover the tested asset. The HIFLD hub URL returned 404 here; it is not a verified fallback.

## 3. Wikidata and Commons: extend, do not duplicate, the existing pool

**Minimum:** bbox, rough class or landmark name. Existing refs already have SPARQL class/P18/bbox and Commons geosearch. The independent addition is a **provenance expansion workflow**:

1. Start with either a bounded class query or coordinate geosearch, not a worldwide subclass traversal.
2. Expand Commons file titles using `prop=imageinfo` and `iiprop=url|timestamp|user|extmetadata`; retain file description URL, author, credit, source and licence fields where present. `timestamp` is file-upload metadata, not automatically shutter time.
3. Inspect the file description and original source, then related categories/other views. Include geotagged photos whose subjects lack Wikidata P18. Compare camera coordinates versus depicted-object coordinates instead of assuming they coincide.
4. Paginate all returned batches, deduplicate by file/item IDs and report unphotographed items separately. If queries time out, split bboxes, reduce subclass expansion, and retrieve labels after IDs.

[Geosearch API](https://www.mediawiki.org/wiki/API:Geosearch), [Imageinfo](https://www.mediawiki.org/wiki/API:Imageinfo), [WDQS limits](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:SPARQL_query_service/query_limits); captures [geosearch](sources/extended-commons-api.txt), [metadata](sources/extended-commons-imageinfo.txt), [limits](sources/extended-wikidata-query.txt). Geosearch radius has a documented maximum of 10,000 m; do not silently treat one circle as a whole region.

Public APIs need no upload or paid plan; use an identifying User-Agent and honor retry/limit responses. Wikidata structured data is CC0, while Commons files have **individual** licences/attribution obligations, not a blanket CC0 licence. [Wikidata licensing](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing), [Commons reuse](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Reusing_content_outside_Wikimedia); captures [WD](sources/extended-wd-license.txt), [Commons](sources/extended-commons-license.txt). Sparse geotags and missing P18 are unsearched coverage, not contradictory evidence.

For **roads**, a readable road/exit/bridge identifier should trigger the relevant road authority's official route log, bridge inventory, sign manual and works notices, rather than another geolocation model. This is a workflow recommendation, not a claim of verified global road-authority coverage. In this research, municipal signal/lighting inventories and FAA/FRA/NOAA sources above are the concrete verified infrastructure additions. For marine tracks, `marinecadastre.gov/ais/` returned an uninformative shell: AIS download/access/retention was not verified, so do not promise a vessel's historical route.

## 5. OSM history and non-Overpass tooling

### Historical Overpass and ohsome

**Minimum:** bounded AOI, tag/class and date interval. Overpass QL supports date snapshots and historical/difference constructs on compatible history-capable instances. These can recover a renamed station, removed rail spur or mapped construction site. Inspect response timestamps and completeness; instance support and history coverage matter. [Overpass QL](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Overpass_API/Overpass_QL), [capture](sources/extended-overpass-history.txt).

**ohsome** provides OpenStreetMap history statistics/extractions as a separate service. Important discovered change: the requested v1 documentation redirected to **v2**, whose landing page requires signing up for a **free API key**. Do not copy older “no key” recipes. Inspect current endpoint/quota docs before use; no key was obtained and no extraction was run. [Current docs](https://docs.ohsome.org/ohsome-api/v2/), captures [redirect/key notice](sources/extended-ohsome-reference.txt), [reference](sources/extended-ohsome-v2-reference.txt). Public free-key access is not a guarantee of unlimited extraction or SLA.

### Osmium local history filtering

**Minimum:** lawful local regional `.osh.pbf` history extract, AOI and timestamp. `osmium time-filter` materializes a snapshot/time interval from history data; ordinary current `.osm.pbf` files cannot recreate missing past versions. This is a valuable outage/privacy alternative after acquiring an appropriately licensed extract: repeated tag/time queries can remain local. [Project](https://github.com/osmcode/osmium-tool), [time-filter manual](https://docs.osmcode.org/osmium/latest/osmium-time-filter.html); captures [README](sources/extended-gh-osmium.txt), [manual](sources/extended-osmium-manual.txt). Tool GPLv3; OSM data ODbL/attribution obligations are separate ([OSM copyright](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), [capture](sources/extended-osm-license.txt)). No installation/download of a large history extract was performed.

**Recovery:** small historical query → split AOI/time → current regional extract for present-day enumeration → history extract if historical state is essential. OSM edit time is a mapper's edit date, not necessarily demolition/opening time. OpenRailwayMap/OpenInfraMap are useful visualizations but share the OSM data source and cannot independently confirm it.

## 6. Local image preparation and multilingual OCR

### Metadata/orientation before recognition

**Minimum:** original file, ideally not a messenger recompression. Existing `exif.py` is not a full forensic metadata view. ExifTool's EXIF tables document orientation and capture/offset-related tags; inspect duplicate groups, GPS, clock offsets and editing metadata without writing the original. A proposed read-only command is `exiftool -a -G1 -s original.jpg` **if already installed**; it was not executed on user photos. Keep filesystem dates separate from capture dates. [EXIF tag reference](https://exiftool.org/TagNames/EXIF.html), [capture](sources/extended-exiftool.txt).

Create explicit derivative variants for the eight EXIF rotation/reflection states only when orientation is ambiguous, then retain the transform used. Do not apply EXIF orientation twice. Existing perspective correction/crops remain useful; neither metadata absence nor a plausible GPS tag authenticates origin. ImageMagick's requested options page returned only an uninformative page here, so no unverified option recipe is recommended.

### Independent local OCR engines

**Minimum:** best-resolution sign/text crop and plausible script/language shortlist. [Tesseract](https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tesseract) supports more than 100 languages and has downloadable trained-data alternatives ([README](sources/extended-gh-tesseract.txt), [trained data](sources/extended-gh-tesseract-language.txt)). [PaddleOCR](https://github.com/PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR) is another multilingual scene-text route ([README capture](sources/extended-gh-paddleocr.txt)); choose a documented recognition model for the script rather than assuming every model covers all advertised languages. Both projects advertise Apache-2.0; verify model/dependency licences separately. Downloads/compute are required, but inference can be local; no cloud OCR upload is necessary.

Use one independent engine on the original and a conservative rectified/upscaled crop; compare **characters and boxes**, preserve alternatives for similar glyphs and original scripts, and search local-language text variants. Engine agreement is useful but not fully independent if both see the same ambiguous pixels. Do not turn autocorrected place names into “read” evidence. CPU/GPU availability, model download size and script coverage can block a particular route; fall back to manual reading or a specialist script model, not invented text.

### Super-resolution is a lead generator, never recovered ground truth

[Real-ESRGAN](https://github.com/xinntao/Real-ESRGAN) describes blind real-world super-resolution with synthetic degradation training; code licence is BSD-3-Clause ([README](sources/extended-gh-realesrgan.txt), [licence](sources/extended-gh-realesrgan-license.txt)). It is an independent optional preprocessing library, not a factual character-restoration service. Proposed use: a local derivative to suggest a sign-reading hypothesis. Always retain the original beside it and verify every asserted character against original pixels or another photograph. GAN detail, face enhancement and sharpened strokes are **not measured evidence**. Upscaling cannot establish letters/features lost to undersampling, blur or compression. No model was installed or run here.

[Docs](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/cdo-web/webservices/v2), [capture](sources/extended-noaa-weather.txt). No token requested. Check code/kernel licences before bundling. |
| **Astrometry.net**: genuine resolved star field with enough stars | Plate solving gives celestial pointing/scale/orientation; with known time, calibrated terrestrial horizon and camera pose it can constrain geographic hypotheses. | **Municipal LiDAR/DSM**: candidate camera point and geometric feature | The Vancouver catalog verifies 2013/2018/2022 surface/point-cloud products. This corrects an overly strong existing `data-sources.md` suggestion that rain/overcast can simply exclude a region. Weather observations/reanalysis generally **down-weight**, not exclude, unless the actual temporal/spatial extent and contradictory phenomenon are tightly demonstrated. A trustworthy contradiction must also rule out a wrong clock/date and local weather variability.

## 8. Reverse search and authenticity: trusted workflow, not trusted labels

**Minimum:** a public source URL or approved non-sensitive derivative. Existing Lens/Baidu/Yandex routes are already present. The additions are provenance-focused:

* Google's **About this image** can expose early uses, context and source metadata; availability varies by region. “When Google first found” refers to image **or visually similar images**, not necessarily the exact asset's creation. Confirm exact bytes/scene and open cited pages. [Official help](https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/14177408?hl=en), [capture](sources/extended-google-about.txt).
* A Lens image search itself is an upload or URL disclosure. Google's [image search help](https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/1325808?hl=en) describes the interaction ([capture](sources/extended-google-lens-privacy.txt)); do not infer zero retention or privacy from search convenience. Search the public source URL first when possible, after approval for disclosure.
* **TinEye** remains a potential independent exact/modified-image route, but its requested FAQ URL returned an unrelated “Where does the name TinEye come from?” article and its privacy page a challenge. Therefore retention promises, free quotas, API price and coverage are **unverified**, not recommended as established facts. Captures [FAQ](sources/extended-tineye.txt), [privacy attempt](sources/extended-tineye-privacy.txt). Use the operator's accessible current terms before an approved search, or skip it; do not route photos through unofficial reverse-search proxies.

## Small bounded rescue playbooks

1. **Generic residential street, city plausible:** municipal tree/pole/hydrant/signal metadata → bounded all-class export → compare asset order/spacing and camera-side geometry → historical same-season street images. Preserve private/unmapped coverage gaps.
2. **Demolished/changed building:** permits in both legacy/successor systems → acquisition-dated aerials → historical maps → archived original caption → invariant footprint/neighbor match. Do not use permit issue date as a hard construction boundary.
3. **Only a bus number/destination:** local-script OCR alternatives → official operator GTFS → stop-code/route/destination join → calendar-valid corridor → stop furniture/streetscape confirmation. If feeds are current-only, explicitly leave historical service unresolved.
4. **Overpass empty/busy:** verify response is complete → historical snapshot if needed → official asset inventory or local regional extract → grid imagery if mapping is thin. Switching to OpenRailwayMap does not create independent data.
5. **Aircraft/harbor/rail puzzle:** identifier OCR → FAA/FRA/NOAA dataset/form → route/window-side/feature geometry → time/sun/tide crosscheck. Missing ADS-B/AIS reception is not evidence of no vehicle.
6. **No useful text, but sky/shoreline:** first narrow by map/terrain → time-offset hypotheses → Moon/star/sun or station tide tests → same-date weather/acquisition imagery. Report surviving alternatives instead of force-fitting one coordinate.
